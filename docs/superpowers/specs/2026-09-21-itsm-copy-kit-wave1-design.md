# ITSM Copy Kit — Wave 1 (First-run & Branding) — Design

Tanggal: 2026-09-21

## Context

Platform adalah fork Plane yang direposisi dari project management ke **IT service management** (lihat `docs/PRODUCT.md`). Services sudah API-backed (`apps/web/core/services/service.service.ts` → `/services/`), sementara Incident/Problem/Change/SLA belum ada route/model — hanya field & filter incidents di Services.

Copy existing masih banyak beraroma project/software development. Temuan pemicu: `packages/i18n/src/locales/en/empty-state.json:80-83` ("Document everything — from notes to PRDs"). Inventaris lengkap ada 8 klaster (empty state, onboarding/tour, metadata & branding, seed data, billing, metrik agile, AI assistant, docs & email).

Wave 1 membatasi diri ke tiga surface dengan dampak persepsi terbesar: **empty states, onboarding/tour, metadata & branding**. Locale `en` saja; locale lain menyusul lewat skill `translate`.

## Keputusan yang sudah diambil

1. **Dual-vocabulary** — label fitur delivery (Work Item, Cycle, Module, Epic, View, Intake, Page, Project, Sticky, Dashboard) dipertahankan; deskripsi & positioning memakai bahasa ops/ITSM.
2. **Honest by architecture** — hanya istilah yang ada kodenya dipakai (Service, Service health, Service dependencies, Service request/Intake, Knowledge base/runbook/SOP/postmortem, Incidents sebagai field Service). Problem/Change/SLA/CMDB ditahan.
3. **Scope** — empty states + onboarding/tour + metadata/branding. Billing, seed data, AI prompt, email, docs = wave 2.

## Voice principles

1. **Operator-first** — pembaca L1/L2 helpdesk, sysadmin, IT manager; bukan product team.
2. **Operational verbs** — run, resolve, triage, maintain, escalate, document, track. Bukan build, ship, plan, launch.
3. **Honest** — jangan janjikan fitur yang belum ada.
4. **Dual-vocabulary** — label delivery tetap, narasi pakai bahasa ops.
5. **ITIL4 as methodology, not ceremony** — "request/postmortem/runbook" sebagai istilah kerja, bukan jargon proses.
6. **Ringkas, sentence case, no fluff.**
